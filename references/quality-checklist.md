# Figure acceptance checklist

- [ ] Record grain and meaning of group labels documented.
- [ ] Original row count, group counts, checksum and required-field nulls checked.
- [ ] No silently dropped/filled observations or altered numerical measurements.
- [ ] All coordinates, groups, mean, sample SD, median, Q1, Q3, min/max verified.
- [ ] Source-specified units only; group sizes and uncertainties clearly identified.
- [ ] No inferred p-values, trends, regression fits, diagnostic accuracy, clinical claims.
- [ ] Matching outcomes share matching scales; no extreme or SD clipping.
- [ ] KDE shape is not interpreted as a demonstrated physical subpopulation.
- [ ] Distinct colours AND shapes where possible, with accessible contrast.
- [ ] Labels, legends and tick text readable at the journal column width and intended phone size.
- [ ] SVG/PDF inspected for vector content and font/text behaviour where relevant.
- [ ] Separate figure and panel captions describe sampling and error bars.
- [ ] Python, dependencies, command-line arguments and audit are delivered.
